Sixty Feet Labs LLC

An independent software company building consumer sports applications for iPhone — designed and written in-house, without advertising, tracking, or data resale.

What we build

Sixty Feet Labs builds consumer applications for people who follow competitive sports closely — the kind of person who fills out a bracket every March, runs a fantasy draft every August, and argues about both for the rest of the year.

Two applications are currently in development and in private beta testing. Both are native iOS apps written in Swift, and both take the same approach: a small, focused product that does one thing precisely, rather than a general sports app that does many things approximately.

We expect to release publicly through the App Store. Until then, testing is limited to a private group and the applications are not publicly listed.

Approach

Built in-house

Interface, typography, illustration, and code are original work produced by the company. We do not incorporate third-party team logos, marks, or other rights-holders' branding into our products.

No advertising, no tracking

Our applications carry no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Small and deliberate

A narrow product built carefully beats a broad one built quickly. We would rather ship one feature that works exactly right than five that mostly do.
